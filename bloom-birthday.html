To the girl who turns any ordinary room into somewhere worth being —

I have been trying to find the right words for you the way you'd find the right flower for a garden: something that just fits, that makes the whole space make sense, but I am just out of words for everything you have done for me.

Yet, I will give my best shot, why? - because you deserve it!

This birthday, I hope you feel what it's like to be loved the way you love other people — fully, quietly, and without keeping score.
